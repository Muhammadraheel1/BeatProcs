Core Productivity Techniques

🍅 Pomodoro Technique

Francesco Cirillo (1980s). Work in focused sprints to defeat burnout.

  • ✅ 25 min deep work → 5 min break
  • ✅ After 4 cycles → longer break (15-20 min)
  • ✅ Reduces mental fatigue & improves concentration
📺 TED: Inside the mind of a master procrastinator + Pomodoro insights

📊 Eisenhower Matrix

Prioritize urgent/important tasks. Stop last-minute chaos.

  • 🔹 Urgent & Important → Do First
  • 🔹 Important, Not Urgent → Schedule
  • 🔹 Urgent, Not Important → Delegate
  • 🔹 Neither → Eliminate

⚡ 2-Minute Rule

From "Atomic Habits" by James Clear: if a task takes less than 2 minutes, do it immediately.

  • ✔️ Beat small delays
  • ✔️ Build momentum for larger tasks

Research & Case Studies (FAST Context)

📊 Root Causes at FAST NUCES: Based on our questionnaires: emotional regulation, perfectionism, poor time management, task aversion & smartphone distraction. 85% of surveyed students reported last-minute cramming and elevated stress. Our findings align with Steel (2007) and Pychyl & Flett (2012).

📖 Case Study A: Final Year Student (Vietnam.vn)

A student delayed IELTS exam prep for 3 years waiting for "perfect block". Cramming led to severe sleep deprivation. Lesson: Start small and iterate.

📌 Case Study B: 'Busy' Student (Makassar State Univ)

Active in organizations—Intention-action gap leads to physical anxiety. Solution: micro-tasking & accountability.

🎨 Case Study C: Design Student (Loughborough)

Research phases delayed more than sketching. Uncertainty fuels procrastination; break ambiguous tasks into atomic steps.
